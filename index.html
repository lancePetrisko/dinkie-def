D I N K I E

The Word for Everything_

noun  /  ˈdɪŋ.ki/

Definition

A versatile filler word used to refer to any object, thing, or item — especially when the exact word escapes you, or when any word will do.

Basically

Like "thing" or "thingy" or "whatchamacallit" — but better. Dinkie fits anywhere, refers to anything, and somehow always lands exactly right.

Use it in a sentence

  • "yo can u hand me the dinkie?"
  • "look at the dinkie!"
  • "what'd you do with my dinkie?"
  • "bro just grab the dinkie off the table"
  • "wait which dinkie are you talking about"

Origin

Originally coined by Ameen, spread through word of mouth, and now a proud fixture in everyday vocabulary.
