D I N K I E

The Word for Everything

noun / din ki /

Definition

A versatile filler word used to refer to any object, thing, or item especially when the exact word escapes you, or when any word will do.

Basically

Like "thing" or "thingy" or "whatchamacallit" but better. Dinkie fits anywhere, refers to anything

Use it in a sentence

  • "can u hand me the dinkie?"
  • "look at that dinkie"
  • "what'd you do with the dinkie"
  • "bro just grab the dinkie off the table"
  • "wait which dinkie are you talking about"

Origin

Originally coined by Ameen, spread through word of mouth, and now a proud fixture of my everyday vocabulary.
